### 🧭 About

I like problems where the hard part isn't writing the code — it's knowing whether
the thing actually **works**. Most of what I build lately has an evaluation step
bolted onto it.

- 🔬 Right now I'm doing **benchmark design for ML research** — building datasets where the correct answer is known by construction, so competing models can be graded instead of guessed at. Hundreds of training runs, paired statistics, negative controls.
- 🎓 Building **PakTutor**, a free bilingual AI tutor for Pakistani FSc and Matric students.
- 🌱 Going deeper on **PyTorch**, experiment design, and making results hold up under review.
- 💬 Ask me about RAG pipelines, ports-and-adapters architecture, or why your R² can be negative.
